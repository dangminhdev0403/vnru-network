# VN–RU Portal Design DNA

## Product mental model
Institutional science and technology network spanning public discovery, authentication, member workspaces, governance, and security.

## Visual direction
Institutional modernism: serious, calm, editorial, high-trust; public brand and operational clarity share one visual language.

## Sources of truth
Current source and `docs/DESIGN.md`/`DESIGN.md` remain implementation truth. This directory is the enforceable AI review contract; it clarifies repeated corrections instead of creating a parallel component library.

## Inventory
- `primitives.md`: colors, typography, spacing, shape, elevation, motion.
- `scales.md`: responsive, grid, density, modes, stability.
- `semantics.md`: role mappings.
- `principles.md`: judgment rules.
- `behaviors.md`: interaction/state/motion.
- `components/core.md`: shared component rules.
- `patterns/surfaces.md`: public, operational, auth, responsive patterns.

## Non-negotiable readability gate
Normal content and primary controls are at least 16px. Secondary metadata is at least 14px. A smaller size requires an accessibility-safe decorative/nonessential exception and must never carry a primary action, error, instruction, or business value.
