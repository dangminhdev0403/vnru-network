Optimization of Biopolymer Extraction from Vietnamese Gracilaria tenuistipitata using Deep Eutectic Solvents

Dr. Nguyen Van A (VNU Hanoi), Prof. E. Sokolova (Lomonosov MSU)

This collaborative study presents a novel, green extraction methodology for marine biopolymers. Utilizing specific deep eutectic solvents (DES), the extraction yield from Gracilaria tenuistipitata was improved by 42% compared to traditional hot water extraction, while preserving the structural integrity of the polysaccharides. The findings offer significant implications for sustainable biomaterial production...
